Frost Tower Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026 · Developer: Raven Tobias

In short: Frost Tower does not collect, send, sell or share any personal information. Your best score, floor, coins and settings stay on your own device.

This policy explains what the mobile game Frost Tower ("the app"), made by Raven Tobias, does with information. Frost Tower is a single-player offline game.

1. Information we collect

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDKs and no in-app purchases. The app does not request the Internet permission.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device only, using Android's local app storage:

  • your best score and highest floor reached
  • coins collected and number of games played
  • your sound and vibration settings

This data never leaves your device. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

3. Children

The app contains no ads, no chat, no links out of the app and collects no data, so it is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

4. Third-party services

None are integrated. When you download the app, Google Play may collect information under Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Changes to this policy

If we ever add features that process data (for example ads or leaderboards), we will update this policy and the Play Store Data safety section before releasing that version, with a new date posted on this page.
